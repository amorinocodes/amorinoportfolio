About Me

I'm a UX/UI designer studying at the University of Sydney, drawn to the intersection of user psychology and considered craft. I believe the best interfaces feel less like software and more like a conversation intuitive, purposeful, and occasionally a little delightful.

Outside the screen, I'm the President of the USYD Chocolate Society, which has taught me more about community building, event logistics, and keeping 100's of hungry uni students happy and entertained I'm also Vice President of SUEDE (University Experience Designers), where I help shape how our faculty approaches design education and student experience — work that constantly reminds me why getting design right actually matters.

I'm looking for a team where I can bring both rigour and personality to real problems — somewhere that cares as much about th details as I do.
